South Africa's talent pool is scaling faster than the headlines

South Africa's global-business-services sector added 26,346 new export jobs in 2025, its strongest year since 2018, and is targeting 500,000 by 2030. BPESA / Everest Group

The angle: the pool of Western-market-ready agents you can tap is deepening fast, which is why more regulated work (insurance, financial services, iGaming) is moving to SA, not only to Asia.

03 / 04🧭 Destinations

South Africa vs the Philippines: the trade-off in 2026

The two most-compared offshore CX destinations pull in different directions. The Philippines still wins on raw scale and is marginally cheaper per agent; South Africa wins on retention and cultural fit for Western markets, with agent attrition running materially lower, which quietly narrows the cost gap once hiring and training are counted.

The angle: for a US or UK buyer the real question isn't "which is cheaper per seat," it's "which keeps the team that learns my customer."

04 / 04🛡️ Regulated

Why regulated work is the next wave to move offshore

The insurance BPO market alone is set to grow from $68.4bn in 2026 to $93.1bn by 2031. Compliance used to be the reason regulated work stayed onshore; increasingly it's the reason it can move, as buyers choose partners on the strength of their certifications and controls, not just cost. Mordor Intelligence

The angle: the question has flipped from "can we risk it offshore" to "which partner can prove the controls," ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI-DSS.
